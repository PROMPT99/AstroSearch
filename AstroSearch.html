Binary & Variable Star Classification Guide

Reference for SIMBAD / VSX codes when inspecting light curves.

EA (Algol type) Detached Binary

Sharp primary and secondary eclipses. Constant brightness between eclipses. Very common in TESS.

EB (Beta Lyr) Semi-Detached

Continuous light variation due to tidal distortion of ellipsoidal stars. No flat bottom minima.

EW (W UMa) Contact Binary

Overcontact binary stars sharing a common envelope. Equal depth minima and continuous sinusoidal wave.

SB* / Candidate Spectroscopic / New

Spectroscopic binary or uncataloged transit candidate worthy of posting in Zooniverse Talk!
